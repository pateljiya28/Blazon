Our Company

Blazon is a well reputed brand for premium designer wooden wall clocks and gift articles. The organization manufactures and distributes wooden wall clocks, wooden and glass combinations, gift items, and modular kitchen cabinetry.

Established in 2008 in Vadodara, Gujarat, India, we are amongst those foremost companies in India engaged in manufacturing and supplying wall clocks for various settings including homes, offices, and corporate gifting needs.

Blazon is the only company using Italian Membrane process techniques on clock cabinets for better finishing and long life of wooden wall clocks.

Our Leadership

The company is guided by Mr. Renish B. Makadia and Mr. Pankaj B. Makadia, who have built the brand's strong domestic market reputation through dedication to quality and innovation.

Our Expertise

The company has developed internal technical expertise for designing and producing wall clocks and gift items. Our commitment is to fulfill customer satisfaction through products meeting the highest standards of quality, cost-effectiveness, and timely delivery.
